# Design Context — AI/ML History PWA

## Users

Three overlapping audiences:

1. **Researchers / ML practitioners** — tracing lineage, finding foundational papers, understanding how ideas connect across decades. They filter, navigate, and follow cross-references.
2. **Curious technologists** — learning how AI got here. They explore, read summaries, and follow the narrative from Turing to foundation models.
3. **Showcase audience** — evaluating cerebral.work craft. They assess the density, interaction quality, and information design.

The same screen serves all three: experts filter and navigate; newcomers explore and read; showcase visitors assess the visual craft.

## Brand Personality

Scholarly + technical + honest. cerebral.work operator voice — "we built the floor you're standing on." Authority through restraint, not decoration.

## Aesthetic Direction

- Dark mode only (`#0a0a0a` base). No light mode.
- Dual-font system: monospace for data, sans-serif for UI chrome.
- No gradients (except functional data viz), no glassmorphism, no AI-generic aesthetic.
- References: museum exhibit, Bloomberg terminal, cerebral.work website.
- Anti-references: AI slop, academic dryness, gamification.

## Design Principles

1. **Density is the content** — the 23× acceleration in publication rate is the visual story. The timeline must make this immediately visceral.
2. **Every pixel carries data** — no decoration, no ornament, no filler. If it doesn't encode information, it doesn't belong.
3. **Interaction reveals depth** — rest shows overview, hover shows connections, click shows lineage, navigate shows structure.
4. **Monospace for data, sans for chrome** — the font contrast reinforces the data/UI distinction.
5. **Restraint over richness** — boldness comes from contrast and density, not effects.

## Color System

- Base: `#0a0a0a` (near-black)
- Surface: `#141414`, `#1a1a1a`
- Border: `#2a2a2a`
- Text: `#e0e0e0` (primary), `#888` (dim)
- Accent: `#5e72e4` (indigo — scholarly, technical, distinct from AI-green cliché)
- Category colors: hand-tuned per-category, preserved from the dataset

## Typography

- Mono (data): `"SF Mono", "JetBrains Mono", "Fira Code", monospace` — paper titles, years, metrics, tooltips
- UI (chrome): `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", sans-serif` — buttons, labels, panel headings, search
- No external fonts — offline-first PWA purity. The mono/sans contrast is the design point, not a specific typeface.

## Emotional Goals

- **Awe** (primary): the density hit — seeing 140 papers compressed into a timeline where the 23× acceleration is immediately visible
- **Understanding** (secondary): tracing how ideas flow across decades and categories
- **Discovery** (tertiary): finding connections you didn't know existed (lineage, convergence)

## Anti-Patterns (Forbidden)

- AI-generic aesthetic (purple gradients, glassmorphism, neon glow)
- Gamification (scores, badges, progress bars, "achievements")
- Academic dryness (walls of unformatted text, no interaction)
- Decoration without data (backgrounds, patterns, ambient animations)
- Accordion UI (forbidden per project convention)
